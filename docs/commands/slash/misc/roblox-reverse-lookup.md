# Roblox reverse_lookup

Finds the members of your server who verified with a specific Roblox account. This is useful for spotting alt accounts, or for finding a member when you only know their Roblox name.

:::slash
**reverse_lookup** is a [slash command](/commands/info/slash/)!
:::

## Arguments

Please only choose one of the arguments.

- 1 **Roblox_ID**  
    The ID of the Roblox account.

- 2 **Roblox_name**  
    The username of the Roblox account. Display names won't work.

Only members who verified in this server with the [Verification module](/modules/verification) are found. Members who left the server are still listed by their ID.

## 🔒 Required permissions

No permissions required.
