# Mod | History

The History command displays the moderation history of a member, or of your whole server. It shows their warns, bans, kicks, mutes, timeouts and notes, and lets you edit or remove cases.

---
## Arguments

- **1 Member**
    The member whose history you want to see. Leave empty to see the history of your whole server.


---
## REQUIRE 2FA

If activated, your Moderators will need to use Two Factor Authentication every 2 hours to use the command.

Not recommended for smaller servers. But very helpful for large servers that are vulnerable to Moderator accounts being hacked.
