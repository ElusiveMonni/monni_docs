# Tic-Tac-Toe command

Play a game of tic-tac-toe in the channel. Play against a friend by mentioning them, or play against Monni if you leave them out. Monni never loses.

:::info
**tic_tac_toe** is a [prefix command](/commands/prefix/)!
:::

---
## Usage

`@Monni tic_tac_toe @member`

## Arguments

- 1 **Member** (optional)  
    The member you want to play against. Leave it out to play against Monni.

## Aliases

- tic_tac_toe
- tictactoe
- tic-tac-toe

Players take turns pressing the buttons under the board. A game ends after 30 minutes without a move.

## 🔒 Required permissions

No permissions required.
