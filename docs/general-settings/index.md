title: General Settings

General settings lets you configure bot's settings which don't belong to any module or home page section.


## Localisation
You can change the server's language from the localisation section of the general settings.


## Linked Channels
You can manage the existing linked channels. You are limited to **20** linked channels, or **100** with premium.

:::info
Read more at [linked channels page](/general-settings/linked-channels).
:::
