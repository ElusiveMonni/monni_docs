---
Invite tracking is a sub-module of the logging module. It's enabled by default and cannot be disabled. You can choose to log the invite tracking information from the **Logging Module**. 


## Checking Used Invite
In addition to logs, you can see what invite was used by using the `/discord whois` command.



## Rewarding Inviters
Invite tracking is integrated into our **Points Module**, and you can reward members who actively invite new people to your community. See our [guide](/guides/invite-tracking-rewards) on setting this system up.
