# Member joined

The member joined trigger runs every time someone joins your server. Bots joining don't trigger it.

## Options
This trigger has no options.

## Variables
- `{{member}}` | The member who joined, like `{{member.mention}}` or `{{member.name}}`.

Actions that need a member, like giving a role or points, apply to the member who joined.
