Modules are the parts of Monni you can turn on and set up for your server. Each one is switched on and configured from the [dashboard](https://monni.fyi/dashboard).

Our modules are:

---
#### [**Moderation**](/modules/moderation)
Ban, kick, mute, time out and warn members. Every punishment is saved as a case you can look back on.

---
#### [**Auto Mod**](/modules/automod)
Filters for spam, mass mentions, links, blocked words and more, each with the punishment you choose.

---
#### [**Anti Bot**](/modules/anti-bot)
Catches hacked accounts and spam bots that post across your channels, and removes them before they cause damage.

---
#### [**Logging**](/modules/logging/)
Keep a record of what happens in your server, from deleted messages to role changes, in the channels you choose.

---
#### [**Invite Tracking**](/modules/logging/invite-tracking)
See which invite every new member used to join, and who created it.

---
#### [**Verification**](/modules/verification/)
Make members link a Roblox or Minecraft account before they get access to your server.

---
#### [**Roles**](/modules/roles)
Give roles automatically when members join, verify or click a button, and keep roles when someone rejoins.

---
#### [**Points**](/modules/points)
Create your own currency with ways to earn it, shops to spend it in, items and milestone rewards.

---
#### [**Automations**](/modules/automations/)
Build your own commands and actions that run on a schedule, when someone joins, or when a message is sent.

---
#### [**Cross Guild**](/cross-guild/)
Connect your server with others to share roles, verification, bans and moderation.
