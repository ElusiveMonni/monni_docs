**A simple guide on adding new translated languages to Monni!**

_______________________________________________________________________________

To get started with translating, you'll want to register with an account with [Weblate.](https://weblate.monni.fyi/projects/monni/monni-bot/).

Once your account is made, your ready to start.
Head to the [folder](https://weblate.monni.fyi/projects/monni/monni-bot/) named "Monni bot".

Here is our simple guide on adding a translation projects from English to ***(Your native language!)***:
<!-- truncate -->
_______________________________________________________________________________

1. Press the "**+**" symbol above existing languages.
2. Start a new translation by selecting your desired language.
3. Hit "**Start new translation**".
4. Use your translation skills to translate Monni in your selected language.
5. After every translation hit "**Save and continue**" to move on to the next string of words!

**Be aware of some strings containing `$count` or similar sequences or anything that starts with "{}" or this symbol** $.

_______________________________________________________________________________
## If you are having some issues, here is a simple help to most of them:

_______________________________________________________________________________

1. If a word in English has no unique translation to a word in your language, you may get an error because you wrote the exact same word. Just press **dismiss**.
2. Be sure to use correct punctuation, otherwise you'll get an error!
3. Make sure you didn't modify any sequences showed above.
4. Start with a capital letter if the sentence in English starts with one also.
5. Lastly, don't forget to preserve special symbols such as: `** | \ < > # @ $ []
