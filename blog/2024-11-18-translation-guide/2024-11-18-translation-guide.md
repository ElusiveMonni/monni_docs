**A simple guide on adding new translated languages to Monni!**

_______________________________________________________________________________

To get started with translating, first register with an account in Weblate.

After that, enter in folder named "Monni bot" in [Weblate](https://weblate.monni.fyi/projects/monni/monni-bot/).

Here is a simple guide on adding a translation project from English to ***(Your native language)***:
<!-- truncate -->
_______________________________________________________________________________

1. Press the "**+**" symbol above the already existing languages.
2. Start a new translation by selecting the desired language.
3. Hit "**Start new translation**".
4. Use your translation skills to translate Monni in your selected language.
5. After every translation hit "**Save and continue**" to move on to the next string of words

**Be aware of some strings containing `$count` or similar sequences or anything that starts with "{}" or this symbol** $

_______________________________________________________________________________
## If you are having some issues here is a simple help to most of them:

_______________________________________________________________________________

1. If a word in English has no unique translation to a word in your language and you get a error because you wrote the exact same word, just press **dismiss**.
2. Make sure to use correct punctuation, otherwise you'll get an error.
3. Make sure you didn't modify any sequences showed above.
4. Make sure to start with a capital letter if the sentence in English starts with one also
5. Don't forget to preserve special symbols such as: `** | \ < > # @ $ []
